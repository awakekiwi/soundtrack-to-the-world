Privacy policy

Soundtrack to the World · last updated 11 November 2026

Soundtrack to the World is a free web app for discovering and listening to radio stations around the world. You can use it without an account. This page explains what little information it handles.

Without an account

Your favourites, stations you add and your listening history are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing about them is sent to us.

With an account

If you sign in (by email link or with Google), we receive your email address and a user ID from our sign-in provider, Supabase. We store your favourites, stations you've added and your recent track history so they sync between your devices. If you sign in with Google, we only ask for your basic profile (name, email and picture). We don't get your Google password, contacts or anything else.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies. We don't share your information with anyone except the services needed to run the app.

Services we use

  • Supabase: sign-in and storing synced data.
  • Cloudflare: a relay that fetches song titles and some audio streams. It doesn't store your listening.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): the public station directory. Stations you choose to share are submitted there publicly.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.

Radio streams are played directly from each station's own servers, which may log your IP address like any website.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. To have your account and synced data deleted, email the address below and we'll remove it.
